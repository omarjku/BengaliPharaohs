## What Hack-Nation rewards
- No public rubric was found before kickoff. **Read the rules on Discord first** and map every criterion to a moment in the pitch.
- Venture Lab selects for "working prototype, sharp thesis" and "track record, full-time drive". Pitch a company thesis, not a feature.
- Judges come from OpenAI, Meta, Apple and AI startups: expect technical questions and low patience for chat wrappers.

## 3-minute pitch
| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Name, one-liner, the persona's pain with one number |
| 0:20–0:40 | Why current tools fail; why now |
| 0:40–2:05 | Live golden path from `DEMO.md`; "aha" at ~1:15; 15 s under the hood on the hard AI part and the sponsor tech |
| 2:05–2:35 | Proof: a metric, before/after or small eval; who pays; what is real vs. mocked |
| 2:35–2:52 | 12-week venture plan and the ask |
| 2:52–3:00 | One-liner again, names |

Rules: one speaker, one demo driver. Pre-copy every input. Skip logins. Offer a judge one live input if the path is robust. Each person can answer questions about their own half.

## Demo video shot list
- 0–5 s: the "aha" output or a title card with the one-liner
- 5–25 s: the persona's problem and one number
- 25 s–2:10: scripted voiceover over the screen recording; zoomed fonts, highlighted cursor, dead time cut, sped-up parts labeled
- 10–15 s: architecture diagram, hard part highlighted
- Proof shot (numbers or before/after), then 15 s on the business case
- End card: names, roles, live URL, repo
- 1080p, external mic, captions, unlisted link tested in a private window, **uploaded by Sun 13:00**

## Losing patterns to avoid
Scope creep with no working demo · rushed pitch · jargon instead of the user's outcome · no backup when the live demo breaks · a me-too wrapper idea.
